Research Focus & Technical Expertise

Bridging experimental molecular physics, space missions, optical sensing, light scattering, and physics-informed machine learning.

Breath Analysis

Built end-to-end multi-species deep learning pipelines for breath biomarker quantification from FTIR spectroscopy, improving correlation with mass spectrometry from $r=-0.04$ to $r=+0.94$.

Light Scattering & Sensing

Utilized broadband infrared IDFG sources to analyze light-scattering profiles across complex surfaces and developed physics-oriented models for light-scattering simulation and path-integrated trace gas absorption.

Space Sciences & JWST

Lead researcher on James Webb Space Telescope programs (>300 hours across JOYS, ICE AGE, HEFE, NAOMY-J) targeting molecular hyperspectral data to unveil complex organic molecules in space.

Biomedical Data Science

Delivered PyTorch deep learning architectures (U-Net and attention-based models) for anomaly detection in brain-MRI data reaching 95% precision, alongside attention-enhanced U-Nets for baseline estimation.

LIDA Platform

Database / Web

Architected and deployed the Leiden Ice Database for Astrochemistry. Built on a relational SQL backend with automated ETL pipelines and interactive Bokeh visualization frontends. Scaled into the world's largest infrared spectroscopy ice database used by NASA, ESA, and universities globally.

Ref: Rocha et al. (2022, A&A)

ENIIGMA Tool

Genetic ML / Fitting

Designed and developed ENIIGMA, an open framework employing genetic algorithms for automated pattern recognition, feature extraction, and statistical inference on high-dimensional infrared spectra.

Ref: Rocha et al. (2021, A&A)

Physics-Informed DL

Deep Learning

Custom PyTorch pipelines featuring attention-enhanced U-Nets for spectral baseline estimation, physics-informed absorption losses, and multi-class anomaly detection in complex spatial and spectroscopic imaging data.
