Current Research Focus

Bridging molecular physics, light-matter interaction, and data science for medical diagnostics and astrophysics.

Trace Gas & Breath Analysis

Developing high-precision spectroscopic and signal analysis techniques for trace gas detection. Applying molecular sensing algorithms to exhaled breath analysis for non-invasive biomedical diagnostics and volatile organic compound (VOC) profiling.

Light Scattering & Spectroscopy

Modeling trace gas detection derived from light scattering dynamics. Calculating optical constants ($n, k$) and complex refractive index profiles to interpret light-matter interactions in both laboratory environments and astronomical atmospheres.

Astrochemistry & JWST

Analyzing infrared spectra from the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST Ice Age & JOYS+ programs) to map icy complex organic molecules (COMs) and protostellar environments.

Scientific Software & Interdisciplinary Projects

ENIIGMA

Optimization / Spectra

Python package employing genetic optimization algorithms to decompose complex overlapping spectral absorption features into constituent molecular components.

Ref: Rocha et al. (2021, A&A)

LIDA & SPECFY

Molecular DB

The Leiden Ice Database for Astrochemistry. Houses optical constants ($n, k$) and molecular ice data with automated ETL pipelines and interactive web tools (SPECFY).

Ref: Rocha et al. (2022, A&A)

MRI Image & Signal Analysis

Biomedical Data

Applying high-dimensional spectroscopic modeling and signal processing algorithms to Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) reconstruction and non-invasive health diagnostic data.
